Summer School on Computational Imaging

The third ICCP Summer School on Computational Imaging will take place July 31–August 1, 2027, immediately before the ICCP 2027 main conference, at the University of Maryland, College Park. The summer school provides students, researchers, and practitioners from around the world the opportunity to attend hands-on courses on current research topics in computational imaging and computational photography.

As in 2026, there will be fellowship opportunities and registration options available for undergraduate and graduate students, as well as registration options for non-student researchers from academia and industry.

We will update this page in early 2027 to provide more information.

Call for course proposals

New this year, we are soliciting proposals for courses to be offered at the summer school. To submit a course proposal, please complete the submission form.

Course format: Each course will be taught twice, once on each day of the summer school, and will involve 3.5 hours of instruction and a maximum of 25 student participants per day. We encourage proposals that include significant hands-on activities for the student participants.

Funding support: Each selected course will receive up to USD 3,000 in support for school-related expenses—including shipping any necessary equipment, travel, and lodging—and three complimentary student registrations to the main ICCP conference for teaching assistants.

Selection process: The ICCP 2027 organizing committee will review submitted proposals and select 2–3 courses to be offered in the summer school.

Timeline: The submission deadline is January 31, 2027 at 23:59 PT. Decisions will be announced by February 10, 2027.
